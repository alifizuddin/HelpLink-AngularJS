Frequently Asked Questions

Essential information regarding volunteer participation, hours, and roles.

Do I need prior experience?

No prior experience is necessary. All community volunteers undergo our free virtual orientation and receive on-the-ground mentorship from seasoned field leaders.

Orientation modules provided check_circle

Can I volunteer remotely?

Yes! We offer remote opportunities including virtual language translation, online school tutoring, caseworker documentation, and digital media administration.

Yes. HelpLink issues tamper-proof digital certificates, hour verification logs, and official recommendation letters valid for academic or professional credit.
