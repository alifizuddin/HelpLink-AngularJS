Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers to help you access community resources with peace of mind.

check_circle

Who is eligible for assistance?

Any individual or family experiencing urgent socioeconomic distress, food insecurity, homelessness, or sudden income loss is eligible. HelpLink operates without discrimination.

vpn_key

Is my information kept private?

Yes. Your data is strictly shared with assigned caseworkers and verified NGO aid providers directly assisting your request. We never share details commercially.

group_add

Can I request on behalf of others?

Yes. You can submit aid requests for neighbors, elderly relatives, or community members in need. Simply specify their location and situation in the form above.
