What is SDG 1: No Poverty?

Sustainable Development Goal 1 is a global commitment to end poverty in all its forms everywhere by 2030. It aims to ensure equal access to economic resources, basic services, property rights, and social protection for all — especially the poor and vulnerable.

HelpLink contributes to this mission by bridging the gap between people in need and life-changing resources. From food assistance and emergency shelter to job training and community support programs, we make it easier to find and access the help that matters most.

Key Targets We Support:

  • Eradicate extreme poverty for all people everywhere
  • Implement social protection systems for all
  • Ensure equal rights to economic resources
  • Build resilience of the poor to climate shocks
