What is SDG 1: No Poverty?

Sustainable Development Goal 1 (SDG 1) is a global commitment to end poverty in all its forms everywhere. It aims to ensure equal access to resources, services, and opportunities — especially for the most vulnerable.

Our platform contributes to this mission by connecting people in need with life-changing resources, from food assistance and shelter to job opportunities and community support. Together, we can help reduce poverty and uplift communities.
